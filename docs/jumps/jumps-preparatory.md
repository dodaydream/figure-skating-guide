# {{ $frontmatter.title }}

The first jumps in Chapter 8 of *The Complete Book of Figure Skating* ask for almost no rotation at all. The **bunny hop** has none — it is a forward skip with a two-part landing. Everything after it is a **half jump**: one half turn in the air, most always from backward to forward, landed on a toe and stepped out of. These are the jumps that teach the action and timing of the full-rotation jumps before the rotation is added — the framework for all of them is in [Jumping Fundamentals](./jumps-fundamentals.md).

::: tip Why half jumps first
A half jump is so named because **one half turn is executed in the air before landing**. An ideal way to begin learning jumps is to start with a few half jumps before beginning jumps with full rotation. You do not need to learn all of them at the beginning — they add interest and variety, both for beginning skaters and later in programs where choreography or a musical accent is appropriate.
:::

::: info Which rotation are these pages written for?
All descriptions in the book — and in all six Jumps files — assume a skater rotating **left / counter-clockwise**. Skaters who rotate to the right reverse the starting foot and the direction throughout.
:::

## Bunny Hop {#bunny-hop} <Badge type="tip" text="First jump · no rotation" />

A great beginning jump — it has **no edges and no rotation**, and gives you the comfort of a two-part landing. It is a bit like a little skip: it takes off from the right foot, steps on the left toe, and finishes on the right foot. It can equally well begin with the left foot.

![Bunny hop sequence: (a) left leg swings backward, (b) extends forward, (c) right foot rocks to the toe for pushoff, (d) right forward glide](/jumps-preparatory-assets/fig82-bunny-hop.png)
*From the book — Figure 8.2: (a) the left leg swings backward, then (b) extends forward; (c) the right foot rocks to the toe for pushoff, (d) followed by a right forward glide*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Body** | Preparation is a two-footed forward glide when the jump is first introduced; as confidence increases, begin with a one-footed glide. The left foot picks up and swings first backward, then through to the front, propelling the forward motion. |
| **Feet** | Right foot rocks to the toe for liftoff. The left toe lands first, followed quickly by a right forward glide — the exchange of the left and right feet simulates a **scissor action**. |
| **Blade** | None noted: this is a forward jump, not done on a curve, so no edge is involved. |

#### Tips

- First exercise: a two-footed jump from standing, with a spotter close by — bend both knees, small jump straight up, and land on the **toe picks of both blades**, just to get comfortable leaving and returning to the ice.
- Rehearse a walkthrough at the rink barrier, holding on with one hand.
- Visualise **stepping over a puddle**.

## Mazurka {#mazurka} <Badge type="info" text="½ turn" />

The mazurka feels like a **sideways bunny hop**: the takeoff and the scissoring free-foot action are the same, but where the bunny hop's body position is square to the print, the mazurka's is **parallel — sideways**.

![Mazurka sequence: (a) preparation, (b) scissor action in the air with feet crossed, (c) landing](/jumps-preparatory-assets/fig83-mazurka.png)
*From the book — Figure 8.3: (a) preparation, (b) scissor action in the air, and (c) landing*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Body** | Begin with a **left forward outside three**; from the back edge of the three, step onto a right back outside edge with the body facing parallel to the direction of movement. In the air the arms are held out to the side, head erect, back straight. An alternate method takes off from a back inside edge. |
| **Feet** | The left toe is placed into the ice; weight transfers from the right back outside edge to the left toe, the left knee bends then straightens to elevate the body, and the right (free) foot swings **across the left in a scissor action — feet actually crossed in the air, right foot in front, left behind**. Land on the left toe, followed by a quick right forward inside edge. |
| **Blade** | Takeoff side: right back outside edge into the left toe pick. Exit: right forward inside edge, often followed by a **forward inside three turn**, which helps control the awkwardness of the forward inside landing. |

#### Tips

- Practice drill: from a standstill, turn sideways and practise the scissoring action stepping **left, right, left**.
- Once mastered, the arm positions in the air can be creatively varied — Olympic champion Tenley Albright even crossed her free foot **behind** instead of in front.

## Ballet Jump {#ballet-jump} <Badge type="info" text="½ turn" />

Named for its beautiful position in the air — traditionally the **left arm overhead, right arm out to the side, skating foot straight beneath the body, free leg out to the side**. The air position can be varied.

![Ballet jump: (a) preparation on the right back outside edge with left toe placed, (b) the ballet air position](/jumps-preparatory-assets/fig84-ballet-jump.png)
*From the book — Figure 8.4: (a) preparation, and (b) air position*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Body** | From a right back outside edge, body facing parallel to the print. As the left toe is placed, the left knee bends so the body can be lifted into the air, and the ballet position is **instantly struck** in the air before landing. |
| **Feet** | Left toe placed for the takeoff; landing is on the left toe, stepping out to the right forward inside edge. |
| **Blade** | Entry: right back outside edge. Exit: right forward inside edge — as with the mazurka, an **inside three turn on the exit** helps control the forward inside landing. |

#### Tips

- **Walk through the jump before trying it at speed.**
- Once mastered, the arm positions in the air can be creatively varied.

## Half Flip Hop {#half-flip-hop} <Badge type="info" text="½ turn · prepares the flip" />

A preparatory jump for the flip: a **half turn** where the flip is a whole turn. Takeoff and landing are on the **same foot** — that is what makes it a hop.

![Half flip hop sequence: (a) vault off the right toe, (b) half turn, (c) land on the right toe, (d) left forward glide](/jumps-preparatory-assets/fig85-half-flip-hop.png)
*From the book — Figure 8.5: the half flip hop (a) vaults off the right toe, (b) rotates one half turn, (c) lands on the right toe, and (d) finishes with a left forward glide*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Body** | Begin with a **left forward outside three**. The feet are drawn together as the body lifts into the air and rotates one half turn. |
| **Feet** | The **right toe** is placed on the ice for the takeoff — and the jump lands on the **right toe**, then the left foot steps forward. |
| **Blade** | Exit: left foot steps forward on an **outside edge**. |

#### Tips

- Shared drill with the half flip: hold the rink barrier and practise the takeoff-toe and landing action.

## Half Flip {#half-flip} <Badge type="info" text="½ turn · prepares the flip" />

The half flip differs from the hop in one thing: **takeoff and landing are on opposite feet**, whereas the hop is executed on the same foot. Everything else — the three-turn entry, the toe placement, the half turn with feet drawn together — is the same.

![Half flip sequence: (a) takeoff from the right toe, (b) half turn, (c) step forward onto a right foot glide](/jumps-preparatory-assets/fig86-half-flip.png)
*From the book — Figure 8.6: the half flip (a) takes off from the right toe, (b) rotates one half turn, lands on the left toe, and (c) steps forward onto a right foot glide*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Body** | Begin with a **left forward outside three**. The feet are drawn together as the body lifts and rotates one half turn, just as in the half flip hop. |
| **Feet** | The **right toe** is placed for the takeoff; the jump lands on the **left toe**, then the right foot steps forward. |
| **Blade** | Exit: right foot steps forward on an **inside edge** — the mirror image of the hop's outside-edge exit. |

#### Tips

- Shared drill with the half flip hop: hold the rink barrier and practise the takeoff-toe and landing action.

## Half Loop {#half-loop} <Badge type="info" text="½ turn · edge jump" />

The only half jump here with no toe pick: it begins moving backward on a **left back outside edge**, rotates one half turn in the air, and lands backward on a **right back inside edge**.

![Half loop panels: (a) takeoff position on the left back outside edge, (b) the half turn, (c) landing on the right back inside edge](/jumps-preparatory-assets/fig87-half-loop.png)
*From the book — Figure 8.7: the half loop (a) takes off from a left back outside edge, (b) rotates one half turn, and (c) lands on a right back inside edge*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Body** | Following a **right forward outside three turn**, push off onto a left back outside edge on a well-bent knee, body **square over the takeoff edge** — do not push the skating hip too far into the circle. Right arm in front, left arm behind. As the left knee straightens for takeoff, the left arm moves toward the right; the arm rotation continues through the jump so the **left arm finishes forward and the right arm back** at the landing. |
| **Feet** | The right (free) foot starts **in front over the print** and **remains in front** during the jump, in preparation for the landing. |
| **Blade** | Takeoff: left back outside edge. Landing: right back inside edge — backward to backward, unlike the other half jumps. |

#### Tips

- **Practise a two-footed landing before attempting to land on one foot.**

## Falling Leaf {#falling-leaf} <Badge type="info" text="½ turn · open position" />

Begins moving backward on a **right back outside edge** and rotates one half turn in the air **with the legs in an open position**, landing on the left toe and stepping forward onto a right inside edge.

![Falling leaf air position: legs open in a near-split in the air](/jumps-preparatory-assets/fig88-falling-leaf.png)
*From the book — Figure 8.8: air position in the falling leaf jump (air-position shot only — the book shows no entry or landing panel for this jump)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Body** | Starting from a **left forward outside three turn**, push off onto a right back outside edge on a well-bent knee. Left arm in front, right arm behind. From the well-bent right knee the body jumps **straight into the air**. |
| **Feet** | The left (free) foot starts in front over the print, then **sweeps from the forward position into an open or nearly split position** before landing. Land on the left toe. |
| **Blade** | Takeoff: right back outside edge. Exit: left toe, followed by the **right forward inside edge**. |

#### Tips

- **Practise the mechanics of the jump until comfortable before adding the open position.**

## Half Lutz Hop {#half-lutz-hop} <Badge type="info" text="½ turn · prepares the Lutz" />

The same as the half flip hop except for the takeoff edge: a **back outside** edge instead of a back inside. Same-foot landing — right toe to right toe.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Body** | Begins from **left back crossovers**; the left back outside edge is held, then the feet are drawn together as the body lifts into the air and rotates one half turn. |
| **Feet** | The **right toe** is placed on the ice for the takeoff, and the jump lands on the **right toe**, then steps onto a left forward outside edge. |
| **Blade** | Entry edge: left back outside (held from the crossovers). Exit: **left forward outside edge**. |

#### Tips

- Shared drill with the half Lutz: before executing, hold the rink barrier and practise the takeoff-toe and landing action.

*No dedicated single-technique video verified for this jump yet.*

## Half Lutz {#half-lutz} <Badge type="info" text="½ turn · prepares the Lutz" />

The same as the half flip except for the takeoff edge: a **back outside** edge instead of a back inside. Opposite-foot landing, like the half flip.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Body** | Begin from **left back crossovers**, holding the left back outside edge. The feet are drawn together as the body lifts into the air and rotates one half turn. |
| **Feet** | The **right toe** is placed on the ice for the takeoff; the jump lands on the **left toe**, then the right foot steps forward. |
| **Blade** | Entry edge: left back outside (held from the crossovers). Exit: **right forward inside edge**. |

#### Tips

- Shared drill with the half Lutz hop: before executing, hold the rink barrier and practise the takeoff-toe and landing action.

## All nine at a glance

| Jump | Rotation | Takeoff | Lands on | Then steps to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bunny Hop** | none | Right foot rocks to toe (moving forward) | Left toe | Right forward glide |
| **Mazurka** | ½ (sideways) | Left toe, from RBO edge | Left toe | Right forward inside edge |
| **Ballet Jump** | ½ (sideways) | Left toe, from RBO edge | Left toe | Right forward inside edge |
| **Half Flip Hop** | ½ | Right toe (from LFO three) | **Right toe** (same foot) | Left forward outside edge |
| **Half Flip** | ½ | Right toe (from LFO three) | **Left toe** (opposite foot) | Right forward inside edge |
| **Half Loop** | ½ | Left back outside edge — no toe | Right back inside edge | — (lands backward) |
| **Falling Leaf** | ½, legs open | Right back outside edge | Left toe | Right forward inside edge |
| **Half Lutz Hop** | ½ | Right toe (from LBO edge) | **Right toe** (same foot) | Left forward outside edge |
| **Half Lutz** | ½ | Right toe (from LBO edge) | **Left toe** (opposite foot) | Right forward inside edge |

The pattern to remember: **hop = same foot, jump = opposite foot**, and the flip pair enters from an inside-edge three turn while the Lutz pair enters holding a back outside edge from crossovers.
