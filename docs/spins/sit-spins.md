# Sit Spins

Sit spins are the [shoot-the-duck](/freestyle-moves.md#shoot-the-duck) adapted into a spin — a forward shoot-the-duck executed while rotating. Muscular strength is needed not just to hold the sit position, but to **rise up out of it**, and mastering the shoot-the-duck first develops exactly that strength and balance. Master the [Spinning Fundamentals](./spins.md) first: centred entry, spin position, pull-in and exit all carry over.

Two spins, one on each foot:

- [Forward Sit Spin](#forward-sit-spin) — the basic sit spin, on the left foot
- [Back Sit Spin](#back-sit-spin) — the same position on the right foot, balanced on the ball of the foot

> **Reading the feet diagrams:** three keyframes — **1 · Entry, 2 · The sit, 3 · Exit** — looking down at your own feet. The entry print spirals into the centre as it does for the upright spins; in the sit stage the dashed free boot is stretched **straight out in front** of the spinning boot. The red dot marks the edge doing the work; pale blue is the print the blade leaves on the ice.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Body** | Back straight but bent forward at about a **60° angle** to the free leg; head up. Arms begin out to the side, then come forward, **reaching out to the ankle of the free foot**. |
| **Feet** | Enter from back crossovers: hold the last right back inside edge with the left (free) leg extended behind, in line with the skating foot; step into the circle on a strongly curving **left forward outside edge**, knee well bent, weight over the skating hip. As the right (free) leg swings around from back to front, the skating knee bends **fully** into the sit. The free foot stays straight out in front — hip to toe in one straight line, **parallel to the ice and level with the bent skating knee**, foot **turned out**. |
| **Blade** | The skating knee deepens from back to front as the three hooks the spin — balance forward, catching the first tooth of the toe pick on a **back inside edge**. |
| **Exit** | Lean slightly forward while bringing the right (free) leg around in a circular motion and **rising up on the left (skating) knee** — the book's image is climbing a lighthouse's circular stairs (below). When fully erect the right foot is in front and the left knee nearly straight; pull in and finish **exactly as a forward scratch spin**. |

**Tips**

- **Climb the lighthouse stairs:** the free leg doesn't actually move on the way up — picture standing in the basement of a lighthouse and climbing its circular stairs to the top. Your body rises *around* the free leg until you're upright again.
- The key exercise is one-leg lower-and-rise strength: supervised off-ice squats and lunges, or the bend-and-rise at the rink barrier with the free leg in front and the weight forward — the same exercise as for the lunge and the shoot-the-duck. **No more than five reps per foot**; overdoing it risks muscle strain.

_No dedicated back sit spin tutorial exists on the preferred channels — it appears only inside compilations and change-foot lessons, so this card follows the disclosed-line practice used elsewhere in the guide._

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Body** | The skating knee bends **fully** into the sit; back straight, bent forward at about a **60° angle** to the free leg. Arms begin out to the side, then come forward, **reaching out to the ankle of the free foot**. |
| **Feet** | The left (free) foot stays straight out in front — ideally hip to toe in one straight line, **parallel to the ice and level with the bent skating knee**, foot **turned out**. |
| **Blade** | Balanced over the **ball of the foot — not the toe pick**. |
| **Entry 1 — from a forward open spin** | From the forward open spin position, the right (free) foot moves **across in front** of the left foot; the left foot pushes off onto first the toe, then the ball, of the right foot on a **right back outside edge**; then lower into the back sit. |
| **Entry 2 — from a three (advanced)** | Turn a **right forward inside three** with the left (free) foot behind the right (skating) foot. As the three completes, the **body turns in toward the foot** — the foot does not turn toward the body — then lower into the back sit. |
| **Exit** | Lean slightly forward, **keeping the left (free) leg in front**, and rise up on the right (skating) knee. When fully erect the left foot is in front and the right knee nearly straight; pull in and finish **exactly as a back scratch spin**. |

**Tips**

- Same strength exercise as the forward sit: bend-and-rise at the barrier with the free leg in front, weight forward, **no more than five reps per foot**.
- The balance point is the whole difference from the forward sit: ball of the foot, never up on the toe pick.

## Forward sit vs back sit

| | Forward Sit | Back Sit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skating foot / edge | Left, hooks to back inside | Right, back outside (or RFI three entry) |
| Free leg | Right, straight out in front | Left, straight out in front |
| Balance point | Forward, first toe-pick tooth catches | Ball of the foot — **not** the toe pick |
| Exit finish | Forward scratch spin | Back scratch spin |
