# Flying Spins

Flying spins combine jumps and spins: take off from a forward outside edge, fly, and land already spinning. Each forward and back spin involved should be **mastered and consistent** before flying spins are undertaken — and a good foundation in jumping basics is required too. If a landing spin on this page isn't solid yet, go back to its home page first: [Back Camel](./camel-spins.md#back-camel-spin), [Sit Spins](./sit-spins.md).

Three spins:

- [Flying Camel Spin](#flying-camel-spin) — fly, and land in a back camel
- [Flying Sit Spin](#flying-sit-spin) — strike the sit position in the air, land in a forward sit
- [Death Drop](#death-drop) — a flying-camel takeoff into a layout, landing in a back sit

> **Reading the feet diagrams:** three keyframes — **1 · Entry, 2 · The spin, 3 · Exit** — looking down at your own feet. Note the print: the entry curve stops at the takeoff and the spin's hook begins at the landing — **the gap in the print is the flight**. The red dot marks the edge doing the work; pale blue is the print the blade leaves on the ice.

## Flying Camel Spin

_A flying camel takes off from a left forward outside edge, jumping into the air with the body ideally parallel to the ice, and lands in a back camel spin. A 45-degree torso angle in the air is also acceptable._

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Body** | Spiral (arabesque) lean on the entry, **right arm trailing, left arm leading**; shoulders remain **square to the print**. On the takeoff edge the left arm begins to open and, simultaneously and symmetrically with the right arm, strikes a square position in the air, the body lying out parallel to the ice. |
| **Feet** | The right (free) leg stretches around in a **wide circle** during the flight. The landing is on the **right** foot, in the back camel: left (free) leg extended, tension created between the right (skating) arm and the left foot for balance and rotation. |
| **Blade** | Setup as for the camel spin: back crossovers, last right back inside edge held with the left leg extended behind in line with the right (skating) foot, then step into the circle on a strongly curving **left forward outside edge**, the knee bending deeper as the body leans into the spiral. At the takeoff the body weight rocks to the **front of the blade, on the toe pick**, as pressure is asserted against the ice for the liftoff. *(Alternate entry: begin from a right forward inside three, stepping forward onto the left outside edge in a spiral position.)* |
| **Takeoff** | As the left forward outside edge is struck, the left (skating) knee bends deeply into the curve of the takeoff circle, then rises quickly for the spring. |
| **Landing** | On a **right back outside edge**, passing over and toward the incoming edge and outside the circle of the takeoff edge. The right knee is well bent on landing, then straightens for the back camel; the body weight balances over the **ball of the foot**. Creative arm positions only once the back spin is mastered. |
| **Exit** | Rise into an open back spin position and finish **exactly as in a back scratch spin**. |

**Tips**

- **Don't rotate on the ice (book's training tip):** a frequent mistake is rotating a full turn on the ice before taking off. The pressure to the front of the blade and the spring from the knee must be quick, so the rotation happens **in the air rather than on the ice**. The takeoff is on a forward outside edge, not a back inside.
- **Barrier drill:** stand sideways to the boards, hold on with the left hand, and jump to the right back camel position — far enough from the boards to lean forward while still supported. Review back camel spins alongside.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Body** | Airborne, the left knee bends again into a tucked, sit spin position. In the landed sit: back straight but bent forward at about a **60° angle** to the free leg. |
| **Feet** | The right (free) foot helps accelerate the body into the air by swinging from back to front in a **straight, diagonal line** as the body turns counter-clockwise into the spin, and extends out to the front in the sit position. In the landing sit it remains straight out in front — **hip to toe in one straight line, parallel to the ice, level with the bent skating knee** — foot **turned out**. |
| **Blade** | Preparation **identical to the sit spin**: back crossovers, last right back inside edge held with the left (free) leg extended behind in line with the right (skating) foot, then step into the circle on a strongly curving **left forward outside edge**, knee well bent, shoulders level, weight over the skating hip. The landing edge is close to, and just ahead of, the takeoff. |
| **Takeoff** | As the left foot steps forward on the outside edge, the arms and the free leg first extend **behind** the body before thrusting forward for the takeoff: the arms come from behind, drop close beside the body, then stretch forward — the motion is similar to an Axel takeoff ([Chapter 8](../jumps/jumps-fundamentals.md)). The left (skating) knee should be fully bent, then straightened on the takeoff as the body springs into the air. |
| **Landing** | The arms remain open and slightly out to the side as they come through, then come forward for the landing. Once the peak is reached, the left foot extends down to absorb the force: it **lands on the toe pick** and the knee bends to absorb the landing before lowering into the sit spin. |
| **Exit** | Identical to the exit for a [forward sit spin](./sit-spins.md#forward-sit-spin) — climb the lighthouse stairs and finish as a forward scratch spin. |

**Tips**

- Practice the takeoff and landing at the rink barrier: stand sideways to the boards, hold on with **both hands**, and work the timing of the left-foot takeoff, tuck, and landing.

## Death Drop

_The death drop is a combination of a vertical flying camel spin on the takeoff and a back sit spin on the landing — a flying camel / back sit combination completed as one continuous action in the air (see [Combination Spins](./combination-spins.md))._

_No dedicated death drop breakdown exists on the preferred channels — the only item is a one-minute tips clip, and the full tutorials are on non-preferred channels — so this card follows the disclosed-line practice used elsewhere in the guide._

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Body** | In the air the body strikes a **spread-eagle or layout position almost parallel to the ice**. The arms stretch out to the side in the air, landing like a flying camel; **check with the left arm forward and the right arm back**, then pull into the back sit. |
| **Feet** | The right (free) foot swings from back to front to accelerate the takeoff. For the landing, the **right leg drops for the back sit position** while the left leg extends behind the body — momentarily like a back spiral or camel — just before the body rotates into the back sit spin. |
| **Blade** | Setup **exactly the same as for the flying camel** (left forward outside takeoff). Landing: the right (landing) **toe hits the ice first, then settles to the ball of the foot** as the right knee lowers into the back sit spin — all the [back sit](./sit-spins.md#back-sit-spin) instructions apply, including balance on the ball of the foot, not the toe pick. |
| **Takeoff** | As the left forward outside edge is struck, the arms and free leg extend **behind** the body before thrusting forward (the Axel-like motion of the flying sit). The left (skating) knee is fully bent, then straightened on the takeoff as the body springs into the air. |
| **Exit** | Rise into an open back spin position and finish **exactly as in a back scratch spin**. |

**Tips**

- **Train it on the floor first (book's training tip):** practice the three positions and their transitions off the ice before taking the death drop to the ice. Timing is critical.
