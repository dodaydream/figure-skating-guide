# Camel Spins

Camel spins are adapted from the **spiral**: spin in the spiral (arabesque) position — body horizontal, free leg stretched high behind in line with the back. In recent years skaters have varied the camel by grasping the free leg and pulling it into innovative positions; the book covers two of those variations (Catch Foot and Curly) under the forward camel. The [off-ice stretching tip](#forward-camel-spin) below — free leg on a ballet barre — helps every camel on this page. Master the [Spinning Fundamentals](./spins.md) first.

Two spins:

- [Forward Camel Spin](#forward-camel-spin) — the basic camel, with the Catch Foot and Curly variations
- [Back Camel Spin](#back-camel-spin) — the same position on the other foot, on a back outside edge

> **Reading the feet diagrams:** three keyframes — **1 · Entry, 2 · The camel, 3 · Exit** — looking down at your own feet. In the camel stage the dashed free boot stretches out **behind** the spinning boot, in line with it. The red dot marks the edge doing the work; pale blue is the print the blade leaves on the ice.

## Forward Camel Spin

_The basic camel spin, from which many variations have sprung._

> **Book note:** Shulman describes this spin on the **right** foot — step into the circle on a right forward outside edge and finish over the right foot — the mirror of the modern left-foot convention, and of her own sit spin (compare the same quirk on the [One-Footed Spin](./upright-spins.md#one-footed-spin)). | | |
| --- | --- |
| **Body** | Lean forward into a **spiral (arabesque) position** as the entry deepens. In the spin, hold the position firmly: **back arched, arms stretched out, head up, neck extended**. Keep the arms out to the side while learning; once mastered, variations add interest — right arm forward and left arm back, or both hands locked behind the back. |
| **Feet** | From back crossovers: hold the last **left back inside edge** with the right (free) leg extended behind, in line with the skating foot; the free foot then steps into the circle on a strongly curving **right forward outside edge**, right knee well bent, shoulders level, weight over the skating hip. The skating knee deepens as the body leans into the spiral. |
| **Blade** | The three hooks the spin and the skating knee straightens, balance forward, catching the first tooth of the toe pick — then keep the weight on the **ball of the foot on a back inside edge**, rather than up on the toe pick. |
| **Exit** | The left (free) leg lowers and the body lifts to vertical, still spinning over the right foot; the free leg swings around to the front, the arms pull in, and the spin finishes **exactly as a forward scratch spin**. |

**Tips**

- **Stretch for it:** place the free leg on a ballet barre, facing toward or away from it, and stretch the muscles of both the elevated and standing legs — lift the ribcage up and stretch the upper body *before* leaning forward, and always stretch both sides for bilateral balance.
- **Advanced — forward outside camel:** shift the weight farther to the **back of the blade** and spin on a forward outside edge; the left hip and toe lift as the weight moves back.

### Variation — Catch Foot Camel

![Catch foot camel (Fig. 7.13): the right hand catches the free blade behind](/spins-assets/camel/fig713-catch-foot-camel.png)

*From the book — Figure 7.13: catch foot camel.*

From the camel spin position, the **right hand reaches back to catch the right (free) blade** and the right leg bends. The preparation, spin and exit are exactly the same as for the forward camel.

### Variation — Curly Camel

![Curly camel (Fig. 7.14): the left hand catches the right blade, thigh parallel to the ice](/spins-assets/camel/fig714-curly-camel.png)

*From the book — Figure 7.14: curly camel (named by British coach Joan Ogilvie).*

From the camel spin position, the **left hand reaches back to catch the right (free) blade**. The right leg bends, but the **thigh remains parallel to the ice**. Same preparation, spin and exit as the forward camel.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Body** | Torso bent forward in the spiral position through the change of foot. In the spin, the **right arm pulls slightly toward the left free foot**, creating tension and increased momentum. Arms begin out to the side — creative positions only after the back camel is mastered. |
| **Feet** | The left (free) leg stretches high behind in the spiral line, as in the forward camel. |
| **Blade** | **Right back outside edge** — the skating knee straightens and the balance sits toward the **front of the blade**. |
| **Entry 1 — from a forward open spin** | From the forward open spin position, the right (free) foot moves across in front of the left foot, and the left foot pushes off onto the **toe of the right foot** on a right back outside edge as the body leans forward into the spiral. The same front-to-back change as the back scratch spin — but with the torso bent forward during the change. |
| **Entry 2 — from a three (advanced)** | Turn a **right forward inside three**, left (free) foot behind the right (skating) foot; as the three completes, the left foot lifts off the ice in a spiralling counter-clockwise rotation into the back camel position. |
| **Exit** | The left (free) leg lowers and the body lifts to vertical, still spinning over the right foot; the body turns in toward the foot, pulls in, and finishes **exactly as a back scratch spin**. |

**Tips**

- The barre stretching from the forward camel applies here too — both legs, ribcage lifted before you lean forward.

## Forward camel vs back camel

| | Forward Camel | Back Camel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skating foot / edge (book) | Right — hooks to back inside | Right — back outside |
| Free leg | Left, high behind in the spiral line | Left, high behind in the spiral line |
| Balance point | Ball of the foot, not the toe pick | Toward the front of the blade |
| Exit finish | Forward scratch spin | Back scratch spin |
